About us

Built by students,
for students

ExchangeCompass is a free tool that helps university students compare exchange and study abroad programs across the world. We built it because choosing a semester abroad is one of the biggest decisions you'll make at university — and the information is scattered, outdated, or hidden behind expensive paid services.

We believe every student deserves access to clear, honest, student-sourced information about what it's actually like to study at a university abroad — the costs, the city, the campus culture, and whether the academic experience lives up to the brochure.

What ExchangeCompass does

Our platform brings together data on over 548 exchange-eligible universities across 79 countries, covering:

  • Real cost estimates — student residence, shared apartments, food, and total monthly ranges, converted to your currency
  • City and campus scores — nightlife, culture, nature, food scene, beach access, safety, climate, and more
  • QS World University Rankings 2025 — so you know the academic standing of every destination
  • Student reviews — first-hand accounts from students who've actually done the exchange
  • Budget calculator — compare the true cost of living at any destination before you commit
  • Match tool — answer a few questions and get personalised university recommendations ranked for you
  • Exchange timeline — see application deadlines and semester dates for every university
  • Interactive map — explore universities geographically and discover destinations you hadn't considered

How we keep data accurate

Cost data is sourced from official university websites, Numbeo cost-of-living databases, and student-submitted reviews. City scores are based on publicly available data including the EF English Proficiency Index, crime statistics, and quality-of-life indices. QS rankings are updated annually from the official QS World University Rankings.

We continuously audit and correct our data. If you notice an error, you can submit a review and flag the issue — student contributions are the most valuable source of ground-truth data we have.

The review system

Reviews on ExchangeCompass are submitted by students who have attended an exchange program. To keep reviews genuine and spam-free, you must submit a review of your home or exchange university before you can read other students' reviews. This creates a community of contributors, not consumers — every reader is also a contributor.

Reviews cover overall rating, approximate living costs, accommodation recommendations, and first-hand observations about the exchange experience.

Who we are

ExchangeCompass was created by a Canadian university student who wanted a better way to research exchange destinations. What started as a personal project became a full platform after realising how many other students faced the same problem — too many options, not enough honest information.

The platform is independent and not affiliated with any university, government body, or study abroad agency. We don't charge students anything and never will.

Monetisation and transparency

ExchangeCompass is supported by Google AdSense display advertising and affiliate partnerships with travel services relevant to students going abroad. Affiliate links are clearly associated with relevant products (such as travel insurance). We only recommend services we'd use ourselves. Advertising revenue goes toward hosting, data maintenance, and future development.
