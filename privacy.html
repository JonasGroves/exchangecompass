Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how ExchangeCompass ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at uniexchangecompass.netlify.app. By using ExchangeCompass, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Review submissions — When you submit a review, we collect the information you provide: your overall rating, approximate costs, accommodation notes, written review text, and optionally your home university name. Reviews do not require you to create an account or provide a name or email address.
  • Usage data — We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to understand how visitors use our site. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personal identifiers. Data is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Local storage — We use your browser's local storage to remember your currency preference and whether you have submitted a review (which unlocks reading other reviews). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Approximate location — With your permission, we use your device's geolocation to estimate flight distances to exchange destinations. This data is used only in your browser and is not stored or transmitted.

Review data and Firebase

Reviews you submit are stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database service operated by Google LLC. Submitted reviews are publicly visible to other users of ExchangeCompass who have also submitted a review. Do not include personal identifying information in your review text that you would not want to be publicly visible.

Google's privacy policy applies to data stored in Firebase. You can read it at policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

ExchangeCompass uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting google.com/settings/ads.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet. For more information, see Google's advertising policies.

Affiliate links

Some links on ExchangeCompass are affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only include affiliate links for services we consider genuinely useful to students going abroad, such as travel insurance. Affiliate links are marked where practical.

Cookies

ExchangeCompass itself does not set cookies. However, third-party services we use (Google AdSense, Firebase) may set cookies on your device. You can control cookie settings through your browser settings or opt-out tools provided by those services.

Data retention

Review data submitted to Firebase is retained indefinitely to maintain the usefulness of the review database. You can delete any review you submitted from the Reviews page on the site using the My submissions button — no email required. If you have difficulty removing a review, contact us at uniexchangecompass@gmail.com and we will remove it within 7 days.

Children's privacy

ExchangeCompass is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to our site, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of ExchangeCompass after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at uniexchangecompass@gmail.com.
